Reflections of a Physicist

The Father of the Atomic Bomb

J. Robert Oppenheimer was an American theoretical physicist who became the scientific director of the Manhattan Project during World War II. Under his leadership, scientists at Los Alamos developed the world's first atomic bomb.

July 16 1945

The Trinity Test

On July 16, 1945, Oppenheimer and his team witnessed the first successful nuclear explosion in history. The test took place in the New Mexico desert and marked a turning point in modern history.

Science, Power and Responsibility

After the war, Oppenheimer became increasingly concerned about the consequences of nuclear weapons. He spent his later years speaking about the responsibility of scientists and the dangers of an uncontrolled nuclear arms race.
